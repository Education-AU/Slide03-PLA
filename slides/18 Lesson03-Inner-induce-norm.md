title: An inner product induces a norm
template: default
---
First important property of an inner product, is that it generally induces a norm by

Let $v\in V $
$$
\lVert v \rVert = \sqrt{\langle v,v \rangle}
$$
And thereby a metric of course.

In the dot notation it would be
$$
\lVert v \rVert = \sqrt{v\boldsymbol{\cdot} v }
$$
