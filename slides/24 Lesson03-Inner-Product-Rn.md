The standard inner product (or dot-product) on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is as previously stated

Let $\mathbf{u}=(u_1,u_2,\dots ,u_n),\mathbf{v}=(v_1,v_2,\dots ,v_n)$ then
$$
\langle u,v \rangle=\sum_{i=1}^n u_iv_i
$$

Then the induced norm becomes

$$
\lVert u \rVert_2=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n u_i^2}
$$
