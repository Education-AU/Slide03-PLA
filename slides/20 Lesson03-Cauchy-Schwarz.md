Continuing and inserting $\alpha$ we obtain
$$
\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{u} \rangle
+
\frac{\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle^2}{\langle \mathbf{v},\mathbf{v} \rangle}
-
2\frac{\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle ^2}{\langle \mathbf{v},\mathbf{v} \rangle} \geq 0
$$
implying
$$
\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{u} \rangle \geq \frac{\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle ^2}{\langle \mathbf{v},\mathbf{v} \rangle}
$$
and furthermore
$$
\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle ^2\leq \langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{u} \rangle \langle \mathbf{v},\mathbf{v} \rangle
$$
Which produces the result
$$
\lvert \langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle \rvert \leq \lVert \mathbf{u} \rVert \lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert
$$
This very important inequality is used in the verification of the inner product induced norm
