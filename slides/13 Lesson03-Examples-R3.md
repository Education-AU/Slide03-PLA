Consider the vectors $(2,6,2),(6,-2,7)\in \mathbb{R}^3$

<h3 class="h3-blue">Distance using $L_{\infty}$-norm</h3>

$$
\lVert(2,6,2)-(6,-2,7)\rVert_{\infty}=max(4,8,5)=8
$$

<h3 class="h3-blue">Distance using $L_1$-norm</h3>

$$
\lVert(2,6,2)-(6,-2,7)\rVert_1=\lVert(-4,8,-5)\rVert_1 =4+8+5=17
$$

<h3 class="h3-blue">Distance using $L_2$-norm</h3>

$$
\lVert(2,6,2)-(6,-2,7)\rVert_2=\sqrt{4^2+8^2+5^2} =10.24
$$
