title: Orthogonal basis
template: default
---


Consider a finite dimensional vector space $V$ and a basis
$$
\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2,\dots,\mathbf{b}_n
$$
This basis is said to be an orthogonal basis if
$$
\langle \mathbf{b}_i,\mathbf{b}_j \rangle=0,i\neq j
$$

So in $\mathbb{R}^3$ such a basis could be
$$
\begin{aligned}
e_1&=(4,0,0)\\
e_2&=(0,-13,0)\\
e_3&=(0,0,50)\\
\end{aligned}
$$
But the lengths of the basis vectors are not $1$ in the inner product norm $\lVert \cdot \rVert_2$
