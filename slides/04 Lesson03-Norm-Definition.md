A norm denoted $\lVert \cdot \rVert$ on a vector space $V$ over the *complex or real* field $\mathbb{F}$ is defined by
the following axioms

<h3 class="h3-blue">Definition</h3>

1. A norm is a map <br> $\lVert \cdot \rVert:V\rightarrow \mathbb{R}$
2. Positive definiteness <br>$\forall v\in V$ $\lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert \geq 0$ if $\lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert =0 \Rightarrow \mathbf{v}=\mathbf{0}$
3. Absolute Scalability <br> $\forall s\in \mathbb{F},\forall \mathbf{v} \in V, \lVert s\mathbf{v} \rVert = \lvert s \rvert \lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert$
4. Triangle inequality <br> $\forall \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \in V, \lVert \mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v} \rVert \leq \lVert \mathbf{u} \rVert + \lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert$
