Consider a finite dimensional vector space $V$ and a basis
$$
\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2,\dots,\mathbf{b}_n
$$
This basis is said to be an **orthonormal** basis if
$$
\langle \mathbf{b}_i,\mathbf{b}_j \rangle=0,i\neq j
$$
and
$$
\langle \mathbf{b}_i,\mathbf{b}_i \rangle=1
$$


So in $\mathbb{R}^3$ such a basis is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{e}_1&=(1,0,0)\\
\mathbf{e}_2&=(0,1,0)\\
\mathbf{e}_3&=(0,0,1)\\
\end{aligned}
$$
The so-called **standard basis**
