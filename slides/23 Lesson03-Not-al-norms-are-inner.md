title: Not all norms are inner product induced
template: default
---
Not all norms can be induced by an inner product!

Actually a norm **can** be induced by an inner product if the norm satisfies parallelogram the equation
$$
\lVert \mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v} \rVert+\lVert \mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v} \rVert ^2
=
2\lVert \mathbf{u} \rVert ^2 + 2\lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert ^2
$$

The $L_2$-norm is such a norm and is induced by the standard inner product on $\mathbb{R}^n$
