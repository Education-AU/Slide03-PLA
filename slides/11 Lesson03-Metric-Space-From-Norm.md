We can immediately define a metric from a norm.

Let $V$ be a real or complex vector space then

<br>

<h3 class="h3-blue">Metric in normed space</h3>
$ \forall \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \in V: d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})=\lVert  (\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v})\rVert $

All the properties of a metric automatically follow from the properties of a norm.
