As previously stated we will concentrate on the real vector space $\mathbb{R}^n$.

The standard inner product on this vector space is defined as

<h3 class="h3-blue">Definition: Inner product on ℝⁿ</h3>
Let $\mathbf{u}=(u_1,u_2,\dots,u_n),\mathbf{v}=(v_1,v_2,\dots,v_n) \in \mathbb{R}^n$ 

then the inner product is defined by
$$
\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle=\sum_{i=1}^{n}u_iv_i
$$

### Remark:

This inner product is also called the **dot-product** and is sometimes written as $u \boldsymbol{\cdot} v$. We will use the more general bracket notation.
