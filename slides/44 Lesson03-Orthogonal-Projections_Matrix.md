So all vectors in the subspace can be expressed by

$$
Ax, x= (x_1,x_2,\cdots x_k)
$$
Now pick any $y= (y_1,y_2,\cdots y_n)\in \mathbb{R}^n$.

Since we know that there exists a unique projection $\mathbf{x}_0= (x_{1},x_{2},\dots ,x_k)$ of $y$, we have
$$
\langle Aw,y-Ax_0 \rangle=0
$$
for any $w= (w_1,w_2,\cdots w_k)$. Which implies
$$
\langle Aw,Ax_0 \rangle=\langle Aw,y \rangle
$$

In order to perform the last step of these calculations we need some facts about matrix multiplication and inner
products.
