<h3 class="h3-blue">Definition: Complex vector space inner product</h3>

1. The map<br>
$\langle \cdot,\cdot \rangle: V\times V \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$<br>
2. Conjugate Symmetry<br>
$\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle=\overline{\langle \mathbf{v},\mathbf{u} \rangle }$<br>
3. Linearity<br>
$\langle s \mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v},\mathbf{w} \rangle=s \langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{w}\rangle+\langle \mathbf{v},\mathbf{w} \rangle$<br>
4. Sesqui-linearity
$\langle \mathbf{u},s\mathbf{v} \rangle=\overline{s} \langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}\rangle$<br>
5. Positive definite<br>
$\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{u}\rangle\geq 0 , \langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{u}\rangle= 0 \Rightarrow \mathbf{u}=\mathbf{0}$
