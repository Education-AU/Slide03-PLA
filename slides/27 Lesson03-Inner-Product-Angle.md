---
title: Justification of the angle concept in ℝ²
template: default
---

The lengths of the vectors in the $L_2$-norm are
$$
\lVert\mathbf{v}_1\rVert_2=\sqrt{ r_1^2(\cos ^2(\theta)+\sin^2(\theta))}=r_1
$$
So the final formula becomes
$$
\frac{\langle \mathbf{v}_1,\mathbf{v}_2 \rangle}{\lVert \mathbf{v}_1 \rVert  \lVert \mathbf{v}_2 \rVert}=\cos(\theta-\phi)
$$
or
$$
\cos^{-1}(\frac{\langle \mathbf{v}_1,\mathbf{v}_2 \rangle}{\lVert \mathbf{v}_1 \rVert  \lVert \mathbf{v}_2 \rVert})= \theta-\phi
$$
