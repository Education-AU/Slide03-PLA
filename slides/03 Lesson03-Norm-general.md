So far, we have discussed vector spaces in their bare form — that is, without any additional structure beyond the
fundamental vector space operations.

However, in many applications, vectors represent quantities that we may want to 
compare in terms of size, length or distance. 

To do this, we need a way to measure the length of vectors and distance between vectors.

This leads us to the concept of a **norm**, which provides such a length measure and subsequently a distance measure.
