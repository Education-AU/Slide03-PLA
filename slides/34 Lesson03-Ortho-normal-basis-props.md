Does any vector space always have such an orthonormal basis or simpler put an orthogonal basis.

Clearly if we have and orthogonal basis say

$$
\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2,\dots \mathbf{b}_n
$$

we can construct an orthonormal basis by

$$
\frac{\mathbf{b}_1}{\lVert \mathbf{b}_1\rVert},\frac{\mathbf{b}_2}{\lVert \mathbf{b}_2\rVert},\dots \frac{\mathbf{b}_n}{\lVert \mathbf{b}_n\rVert}
$$

all of unit length, so we concentrate on orthogonality
