There are three important points here

1. We can find **the** vector parallel to $\mathbf{v}$ that is closest to $\mathbf{u}$ in the $L_2$-norm
2. We can from **any** $\mathbf{u}$ construct a vector orthogonal to $\mathbf{v}$ such that this $\mathbf{u}$
   can be expressed as the sum of a vector parallel to $\mathbf{v}$ and a vector orthogonal to $\mathbf{v}$
3. Gram–Schmidt<br>
   This construction actually makes it possible from any basis to construct an orthogonal basis. (PLA pp. 149)
